§ 03  —  About

18 years in design. Berlin based, worldwide available.

I started as a print designer and moved to digital in late 2009. Berlin, the HPI bootcamp in 2011, and years of working across European markets shaped the way I work now: simplifying complexity, cautious of easy answers, and insistent on creating sustainable systems for the people using them and the teams building them. Since 2016, I co-lead notallbad and engage in client work mostly as fractional UX leader.

I lead from inside the work rather than from above it: close to the work when it needs me close, stepped back when it doesn't. The work tends to outlast the engagement. Strategy, frameworks, and decision principles I've owned keep shaping product direction long after handoff.
