ERGO Digital Transformation

Replacing EASY, ERGO's nationwide system for insurance agents. Two years. Three organisations. A legacy system used by ERGO's insurance agents that had defined how German insurance was sold for decades. ERGO NEO was a full enterprise digital transformation: PoC and MVP streams running in parallel, three insurance products of increasing complexity, and design infrastructure intended to outlast the project itself.

This case study is currently being written up. If, in the meantime, you need more info about the programme, reach out. I would be happy to share more about the full two-year programme from discovery and design principles through to shipped MVP, usability testing, and production rollout across three insurance products.
